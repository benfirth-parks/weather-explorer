About

Select stations from the list, click map markers, or lasso-select multiple stations. The main trend and wind diagnostics use the main time slider. Snow depth cards below have their own per-station controls and default to 24 hours. Drag any panel's bottom-right corner to resize it. All stations in this app are FTS360 stations. Group 1 is Banff Visitor Safety, Group 2 Banff Fire, Group 3 Jasper Visitor Safety and Group 4 Jasper Fire; only Group 1 is selected on load. Stations above 2200 m are emphasized in wind views by default. See the Avid forecast meteograms for the official forecast.
