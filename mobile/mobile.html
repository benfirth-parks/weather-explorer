About

All stations in this app are FTS360 stations. Group 1 contains default highlighted stations and Group 2 contains the remaining FTS stations. A green dot means live FTS360 data loaded successfully; amber means FTS360 was unreachable and the app fell back to the last known snapshot. Stations above 2200 m are emphasized in wind views by default. See the Avid forecast meteograms for the official forecast.
