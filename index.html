About

Select stations from the list, click map markers, or lasso-select multiple stations. The main trend and wind diagnostics use the main time slider. Snow depth cards below have their own per-station controls and default to 24 hours. Drag any panel's bottom-right corner to resize it. All stations in this app are FTS360 stations. Group 1 contains your default highlighted stations and Group 2 contains the remaining FTS stations. A green dot means live FTS360 data loaded successfully, and an amber dot means FTS360 was unreachable so the app fell back to the last known snapshot. Stations above 2200 m are emphasized in wind views by default. See the Avid forecast meteograms for the official forecast.
